# FLASH STORE V49 – REVIEW MILESTONE STARS

- Review milestone display starts at 2.5/5 illuminated stars, even with zero reviews.
- Each five **published and approved** reviews adds half a star, capped at 5/5 after 25 reviews.
- This is a review-count progress indicator, not the customer satisfaction average. Hover/accessibility label explains the distinction and reports the actual average.
- No Supabase schema change required.
- Deploy to Preview first and verify against live review counts.
